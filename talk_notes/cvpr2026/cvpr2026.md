### Bitter lesson of data, by [Alyosha Efros@UC Berkeley](https://sites.google.com/view/bitterlessonscv#h.sg3lvo14bwzg)
- Biology: Everything is simple once you go meta! -- Darwin
- Physics: Newton to Einstein. No bitter at all. New understqanding should be joyous.
- Humans turned out to be far simpler than we thought
	- 90% we are just nearest neighbor machines, following others
- in bitter lesson, computation ismentioned 21 times, and data is mentioned only once
- Data/Compute trade-off
- What if we want to simulate our world? We need to inject the entropy
- The sweet lesson of data
- 80 million TinyImages: infant sees in first 5 years of life
- img2GPS, data does the driving work. Nearest neighbor is not that bad. 
- Diffusion as texture synthesis
- text2img is also very likely nearest neighbor.
- If you have enough data, pretrain is all you need
- [Neural Thickets](https://thickets.mit.edu/) --> [Video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1HRwXzVEiS)
	- Core finding: The neighborhood around pretrained weights already contains task-specific experts. In small models they are sparse and hard to find; in large models they are dense and easy to discover. --> This tells us why large models are easy to be adapted
	![](./assets/neural_thickets.jpg)
- [Clarke's third law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarke%27s_three_laws)
	- Interpolation/nearest neighbor in sufficiently high dimension is indistinguishable from magic.
- Emergenet capoability: curiosity for better data
	- babies are great deep data miner
- Shallow data vs Deep data 
	- We need more deep data
	- stop scrapping, start mining
